# Automatic proposal validity and turnover clause

The proposal form now pre-fills a standard Terms & Conditions clause:

“This proposal and the quoted professional fees are valid for a period of two (2) years from the date of issue. The quoted fees are based on the client's current turnover and business volume and are subject to upward revision if the client's turnover or business volume increases, or if the scope or complexity of work changes. Any revised fees will be communicated to the client and mutually agreed in writing before they take effect.”

The Valid until date defaults to two calendar years from the date the form is opened. Both the date and clause remain editable. If the terms box is cleared, the standard clause is restored on save. The clause is a suggested commercial term, not legal advice; have it reviewed for your engagement and jurisdiction.

Deploy by replacing the project files in your repository with the contents of this ZIP, committing, pushing, and redeploying on Render.
