# Automatic service scope suggestions

When you type a recognised service name in **Services & fees** or in the proposal builder, ProposalDesk suggests and fills a matching scope of services. The scope remains editable. Recognised examples include trademark registration/search/objection/renewal, GST registration and returns, company incorporation, ROC compliance, income-tax return, DIR-3 KYC, TDS returns, bookkeeping, statutory audit, Udyam/MSME, FSSAI and IEC.

This feature uses built-in templates and works without an external AI key or internet connection. It does not generate legal advice or confirm eligibility. Review and tailor every suggested scope to the client's facts, the exact service, applicable law and your engagement terms before sending a proposal. For a service not in the built-in list, add it to Services & fees with its scope once; it will then be available as a saved reusable service.
